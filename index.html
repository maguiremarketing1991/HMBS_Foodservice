Our Story

It began with a single shop in Navan in 1991, and the standard behind that counter hasn’t shifted since.

01 · FOUNDED 1991

Built on Trimgate Street, Navan

Hugh Maguire opened his first shop on Trimgate Street, Navan, in 1991. Ashbourne followed in 2004. 35 years on, his son Hugh Jnr runs the Navan smokehouse and his brother William runs the Ashbourne shop. The standard set in that first shop has never dropped.

02 · THE CRAFT

Time as an ingredient

Nothing here is rushed. Beef is dry aged for a minimum of 28 days, some cuts for 35 or 40, because the flavour takes that long to build and we give it that time to develop naturally. The bacon is sweet cured to a family recipe and slow smoked over beechwood in the Navan smokehouse. The sausages are made in house, the pork cured traditionally by hand. Even the recipes take their time. Some are researched and tested from scratch, others handed down through the family and kept like heirlooms, because that is exactly what they are. 35 years of that patience, and more than 52 awards to show for it.

03 · TODAY

The same family. A national supplier.

From a single family shop, the business has grown to supply kitchens the length of Ireland: hotels, restaurants, caterers, delis and distributors. What has never changed through that growth is the backbone of it: named Irish provenance, Origin Green verified, on every cut we send. We’ve never set out to be the biggest supplier, only the best one for the kitchens we work with.
